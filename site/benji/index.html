Benji Social

Benji is a red merle Australian Shepherd who is completely obsessed with fetch. Benji Social is the small, private tool his human uses to schedule and publish Benji's posts.

It only works with Benji's own accounts. It isn't a public app: there are no sign-ups, and it never posts, likes, follows or comments anywhere except on Benji's own posts.
